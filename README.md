## 💥 The Problem & The Fix

Autonomous AI agents are incredible at shopping for us, but they are **vulnerable to prompt injection, rogue extra fees, and scam sellers**. 

**TrustLayer AI** acts as a hard security firewall between AI-driven product recommendations and real-world payment execution. We separate AI intent parsing from deterministic product validation—ensuring an AI model *never* has the final say on your money.
